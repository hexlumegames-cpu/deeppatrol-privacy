Privacy Policy

Deep Patrol: Submarine Dive

Last updated: 2026-10-23

Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android game Deep Patrol: Submarine Dive (the “Game”), published by Hexlume Games (“we”, “us”). It explains what information the Game handles, how it is used, and what choices you have.

In short: the Game has no user accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, or camera or microphone data. We do not operate servers or analytics tools for the Game. The only third-party service that collects data is Google AdMob, which shows optional rewarded ads.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress locally on your device: gold, best score, unlocked submarines, the selected submarine, language and sound settings, and daily counters for rewarded ads. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the Game’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the Game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game offers optional rewarded video ads that you may choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When ads are loaded or shown, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following data with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address (which may be used to estimate the approximate location of your device);
  • interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app and SDK performance;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

We do not receive this data. We only receive aggregated advertising performance and revenue reports from AdMob. All data collected by the SDK is encrypted in transit.

Your choices: you can reset or delete your Android advertising ID and turn off ads personalization in your device settings (for example Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your device). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game shows Google’s consent form before ads are requested, where required by law.

Third-party services

The Game relies on the following third-party services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data when you use partner sites or apps;
  • Google Play and Google Play services, which distribute and update the Game.

The Game’s menu contains links to this policy, our terms and our Google Play developer page. Opening a link takes you to a website or app that we do not control.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, please contact us and we will help.

Data retention, deletion and security

We do not keep personal data about you on our own servers. Game data on your device remains until you clear it or uninstall the Game. The Game has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained and deleted according to Google’s policies.

Game data is stored only on your device and protected by the Android app sandbox, and data sent by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit (TLS). However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR and UK GDPR, Turkey’s KVKK, Brazil’s LGPD or California’s CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most of these rights are exercised through the controls described above. For data processed by Google, you can use Google’s privacy controls. You can also contact us at any time.

We do not sell your personal data.

International data transfers

Google may process data in countries other than your own, under the safeguards described in Google’s privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always published on this page together with the “Last updated” date.
